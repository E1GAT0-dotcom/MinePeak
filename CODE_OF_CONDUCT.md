# Code of conduct

MinePeak is a small, friendly project: people climbing PEAK's island together in Minecraft, and helping make it
better. This applies everywhere MinePeak's community meets: this repository (issues and pull requests) and
the [MinePeak Discord](https://discord.gg/URP6bwCxvv).

## Be a good scout

- **Be kind and patient.** Many people here are new to modding, to GitHub, or to English.
- **Argue about ideas, not people.** Disagreeing is fine; insults, mockery and personal attacks are not.
- **Help others climb.** Answer questions you know, point people to the right place, and assume good intent.
- **Take feedback well.** Criticism of code or ideas is how the mod gets better.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, threats, hate speech or slurs, of anyone, for any reason.
- Sexual or violent content, or anything meant to shock.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Spam, advertising, or posting the same thing over and over.
- Sharing or asking for pirated games, cracks, or PEAK's or Minecraft's files.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, or makes you feel unwelcome:

- **On the Discord:** message a moderator.
- **On GitHub:** use **Report content** on the comment or issue (the **...** menu), or message a moderator on the
  Discord.

Reports are kept private.

## What happens

Moderators may remove posts and comments, and warn, mute, or ban people from the repository and the Discord. Usually
it's a warning first, but serious things can go straight to a ban.
